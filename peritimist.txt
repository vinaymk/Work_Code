Scenario:
You are given a list of drugs with prices and need to apply a 10% discount only to drugs that cost more than $100.

Question:

Write a one-liner to filter and apply the discount using list comprehension or a lambda.

drugs = [{"name": "DrugA", "price": 150}, {"name": "DrugB", "price": 90},{"name": "DrugC", "price": 120},{"name": "DrugD", "price": 45}]

--> I want output :

output = [{"name": "DrugA", "price": 135},{"name": "DrugC", "price": 108}]


### Answer discounted = [{"name": d["name"], "price": d["price"] * 0.9} for d in drugs if d["price"] > 100]

### Lambda based solution :
drugs = [{"name": "DrugA", "price": 120}, {"name": "DrugB", "price": 80}, {"name": "DrugC", "price": 150}]

discounted = list(map(
    lambda d: {"name": d["name"], "price": round(d["price"] * 0.9, 2)},
    filter(lambda d: d["price"] > 100, drugs)
))
